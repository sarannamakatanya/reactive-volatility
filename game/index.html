Reactive Volatility

Master the reaction. Survive the consequences.

How to brew

  1. Pick 2 or 3 ingredients, in order. The first sets the potion’s effect, the second its trait, the third its twist. The same ingredients in a different order make a different potion.
  2. Set the flame. A hotter flame means a stronger potion — and a wilder reaction.
  3. Choose a catalyst, or none. Wildspark doubles the power and the danger; Stabilizing Salt steadies it.
  4. Watch the volatility meter. If the reaction runs away, the brew is lost. Stabilize to cool it down, at the cost of quality.
  5. Brew a potion that matches the contract’s effect at the contract’s quality — or better — to fulfil it. Each contract also demands one affinity: match it and earn extra Gems. Ingredients cost 5 coins each (refunded if you take one out). The Creditor takes 100 coins after each fulfilled contract — borrow 50 if you must. Fulfil 3 contracts with no debt remaining to win, before your 12 brews run out.

How to play

Five steps, each one a brew you perform — and every reaction you have discovered, written down.

The five tutorial steps

  1. Order matters. Brew Ember Root + Frost Petal, then Frost Petal + Ember Root. The first ingredient sets the effect, the second its trait — the same ingredients in a different order make a different potion.
  2. Affinity. Brew a Pure pair (the same ingredient twice), a Violent pair (opposites), and a Harmonious pair. Pure ×1.25/+5 volatility, Violent ×1.30/+30, Harmonious ×1.15/+0 — watch the preview for each one's quality and volatility.
  3. Flame. Brew the same pair on Low, Medium, and High flame. Low ×0.8/+0, Medium ×1.0/+10, High ×1.3/+30 — hotter is stronger and wilder.
  4. Catalysts & stabilization. Brew one pair with Wildspark (×1.6/+30), one with Stabilizing Salt (×0.9/−20), then stabilize a volatile brew (−25 volatility for ×0.85 quality). Stabilize is a choice before the reaction resolves.
  5. Contracts. Fulfil a Modest contract (quality 55): the effect must match and the quality must meet the tier. The reward is the minimum quality doubled. A potion that does not match sells for half its quality in coins.
